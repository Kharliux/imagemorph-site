privacy policy.

Last updated: October 14, 2026
Developer: Carlos Rivera · Contact: kharliux@gmail.com

This policy describes ImageMorph for Mac, its Studio trial authorization service, support communications and this website.

Images, documents and device permissions

Image conversion, resizing, optimization, PDF creation and text recognition are performed locally on your Mac. ImageMorph does not send the content of your images, documents, scans or recognized text to the Studio trial authorization service.

ImageMorph accesses the files and folders you select or authorize for a workflow. Camera and scanner features access compatible devices when you use those features and grant the applicable permissions. Workflow settings, processing history, file references, folder-access bookmarks and local entitlement state are stored on your Mac. Local processing records may include file names, paths and error messages.

Studio purchases

Purchases and restores use Apple’s StoreKit and App Store services. ImageMorph receives transaction and entitlement information to determine access to Studio. Apple handles the payment process; ImageMorph does not request your Apple Account password or payment-card details. Apple’s handling of App Store information is described in App Store & Privacy.

Studio trial authorization

When you activate an eligible Studio trial, ImageMorph sends an Apple-signed app transaction and a request-version value over HTTPS to the trial authorization service. The service verifies the transaction and uses its app transaction identifier, app identity and Store environment to establish trial eligibility. This is an account-scoped app transaction identifier, not your Apple Account password or email address.

The service runs on Google Cloud Run and uses Apple CloudKit for durable trial eligibility records. Stored trial records use a server-derived keyed identifier together with the app identifier, environment, record version, trial start date and trial end date. The derived identifier is used to recognize the same trial subject; it should not be understood as anonymous data.

These records support issuing and recovering trial access, enforcing the trial period and preventing repeated trial activation or tampering. Removing the app from your Mac does not itself remove the server-side eligibility record.

Network and hosting information

Online requests expose an IP address and standard connection/request information to the providers delivering the service. Google Cloud infrastructure may process operational and security logs under its configuration and policies. This website is intended for hosting on GitHub Pages, which logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub’s privacy statement and Google’s privacy policy.

This website does not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, cookies, external fonts or an embedded contact form.

Support messages

If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send are processed to respond and investigate the issue. Support uses Gmail. Only send files you have permission to share, and avoid including sensitive content unless it is necessary for your request.

Use, sharing and retention

Information described here is used to provide app functionality, purchases and trial eligibility, respond to support requests and protect service integrity. ImageMorph does not sell this information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking. Service providers process the information needed to deliver their respective functions.

Local files and records remain subject to your device settings and actions. Trial eligibility records are retained for eligibility enforcement and trial recovery, including after a trial expires. Support correspondence and operational logs are retained as needed for support, security and applicable obligations; actual hosting and mailbox settings govern their retention. No fixed automatic deletion period is promised by this policy.

Your choices and requests

You can manage applicable device permissions in macOS settings. You can choose not to activate a Studio trial or email support. For questions about access, correction or deletion of information handled by the developer, email kharliux@gmail.com. Some eligibility or security records may need to be retained to enforce trial limits or meet applicable obligations. Apple manages App Store payment and Apple Account information.

Changes

This page will be updated when the app’s data practices change. The date above identifies the current policy text.
